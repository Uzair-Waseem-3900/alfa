# Profit

- Gross profit = sales - cost of goods sold. Net profit (as this software computes it) = net gross profit (after customer returns) - expenses paid - recurring expenses paid - GST paid - WHT paid - lost cash + found cash - lost inventory + found inventory - depreciation + disposal gain/loss.
- profit_report views: "current_month" is a LIVE provisional figure for the open month (it can change until the month ends) and shows each of those components; "monthly_trend" lists finalized months; "lifetime" gives all-time totals and the split between investors and the owner.
- business_worth = cash + inventory value + assets + receivables - payables - pending recurring dues. Sales-tax and WHT outstanding are shown separately and are currently NOT subtracted from the total. Mention this when giving the worth figure.
- Computed financial figures are not audited statements; say so briefly if the user is making a big decision on them.
